Grupo Requilibrium will live, operate, invest, learn, study and teach with the ideal that we have almost lost; respecting our planet, the environment and the beings with whom we share life.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Requilibrium's philosophy is the result of years of international life and business. It is to become aware of what we do in our world from the point of view of observing a participant in different societies, paying attention to how we work and act. After 20 years of active work and travels through different countries and continents, it took me a few years of meditation and awareness to have a clear picture.

At the beginning of the new millennium, in early January 2001, the whole Requilibrium picture was clear to me. I observed objectively how we have turned our world upside down for at least the last hundred years. We have lost perspective on life and replaced it with all kinds of inhuman and unnatural behaviors.

We are living in a totally unbalanced world. since the appearance of human beings as well as what surrounds us. We have become artificial beings, forcing ourselves to follow behaviors that destroy our interaction with humans and with our planet; ignoring the fortune we have for being part of this universe, thus forgetting the richness of life and what we can have if we understand and learn again how to interact with ourselves.




People who live near the equator, especially the descendants of the Incas, believe to this day that they are: "The protectors of the balance of the planet" and the reality is that we are very far from balance.

Requilibrium is born from the conviction of working to recover the balance and to stabilize what is out of balance. It took us a long time to get out of this balance so we will need a lot of time to balance ourselves again. Educating the next generations is a very important task for us.

As the Incas and the protectors of balance around the equator believe that balance will be achieved when: "The eagle and the condor fly together again."



"REQUILIBRIUM WILL ACHIEVE THAT THE EAGLE AND THE CONDOR TAKE FLIGHT AGAIN CLOSER AND CLOSER TO EACH OTHER."

Dr. Eric Van Der Auwermeulen

COMPANY VALUES

Balance and Harmony

We seek stability and balance in all our actions and decisions, both individually and organizationally. We recognize the importance of maintaining a harmonious work environment.

Resilience and Persistence

We face challenges with determination and perseverance, understanding that recovery and rebalancing may require time and constant effort.

Continuous Learning and Development:

We value learning and training as fundamental pillars for our individual and collective growth. We foster a culture of constant improvement.

Collaboration and Teamwork:

We recognize that working together and collaborating are essential to achieving our goals. We value and respect the contributions of each team member.
